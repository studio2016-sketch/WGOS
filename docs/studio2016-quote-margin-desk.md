# Studio2016 quote and margin desk

## Pilot scope

The source-review panel and quote worksheet live under existing WGOS Admin → Contract Pursuit, using the route's current Studio2016 membership check. The original pursuit tracker and all records remain unchanged.

1. Paste a small excerpt from a rider or solicitation and label it with the page, exhibit or addendum. The offline candidate finder highlights literal lines. It is not a model and does not infer requirements. Verify inclusions and exclusions against the complete official source.
2. Copy selected evidence into the draft's source notes. Fill in scope, date, known cost lines, target margin, and planned price. Missing costs are not zero. Use zero only when documented.
3. Check crew, owned and available inventory, site and logistics manually. Do not assume venue-supplied production hardware or rented core gear. Itemize mandatory venue-provided services, where they apply. A manual check is not an approval.
4. Compare to net collections and reconciled incurred/payable direct costs, without sales tax or duplicate costs. Do not subtract company overhead here and again in the investment model.
5. Copy the internal draft only to an approved private workspace if needed. No save, contact, AI call, send, session storage or database mutation is included. Reloading clears the tab.

## Before an AI pilot

Run the retrospective evaluator on 20 or more approved, de-identified completed jobs; see [the evaluation guide](quote-margin-retrospective.md). Only the synthetic fixture has been run here. A provider integration, persistent drafts, public lead monitoring, accepted change-order capture and deployment are separate steps and are not active.

A future model must emit source pointers to the provided text, never rates, readiness or approvals. Require a positive match to the literal source, a human interpretation and review, a deterministic rate card, an explicit shared-data policy and no autonomous sends. Evidence mismatches, conflicts, unverified exceptions or unavailable inventory must stop approval. Follow the user's separate approval boundaries for deployment, migrations and any external data transfer.
