# WGOS Catalog Manager

Entity-scoped product and service records: SKU, name, description, category, image URL, stock quantity, wholesale cost, regular price, sale price, availability, and status.

Owner experience: one simple form, live image preview, auto margin calculation, draft save, and publish approval. All costs private. Each catalog item belongs to one business and uses a unique SKU within that business.

For retail: maintain inventory movement ledger, incoming stock, sales, returns, and reconciled on-hand counts. For services: scheduling/capacity, never stock count. Do not publish unverified product availability.

Requirements before launch: protected authentication and brand access, database migration, image storage with authorized uploads, audit events, tests for invalid pricing and entity isolation, storefront sync, and Stripe reconciliation. Pending Neon quota resolution; no public editor.
