# Dionne's Boutique — proposed final-sale policy (approval required)

## Customer-facing draft

**All Sales Final — No Returns or Refunds**

Please review your order carefully before completing checkout. Dionne's Boutique does not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, incorrect selections, or unwanted items, and does not offer discretionary refunds.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or incomplete, contact us promptly with your order number and photographs when available so we can review and resolve the issue. This policy does not limit any rights or remedies required by applicable law. Orders that cannot be fulfilled will be canceled and refunded for the unfulfilled amount.

## Checkout and order confirmation requirements
- Display a prominent 'All Sales Final' notice before payment; link to the complete policy.
- Preserve the policy version and acknowledgment timestamp with the order.
- Include the policy in the order confirmation and on the storefront policy page.
- Do not claim all refunds are legally impossible or override payment network dispute rights.

## Inventory workflow impact
- Remove self-service discretionary returns and exchanges.
- Retain administrator-controlled exception handling for damaged/defective/incorrect/missing items, legal requirements, unfulfilled orders, fraud and payment disputes.
- Cancellation before fulfillment: release reservation; if payment was captured and cancellation is approved, issue applicable refund.
- Refund after fulfillment: do not restock automatically. Restock only on verified physical receipt and condition assessment.
- Maintain immutable adjustment and refund audit trails, tenant-scoped permissions and approval controls.

## Release gates
- Obtain owner approval of exact wording and confirm governing jurisdiction and merchandise categories.
- Verify consumer protection, shipping, card network and marketplace requirements.
- Implement policy display, checkout acknowledgment, order records and customer-service exception queue.
- Test exception handling, cancellation, payment failures, duplicate events and refund reconciliation.
- Do not publish this draft or change live checkout until approved.
